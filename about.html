ABOUT ME

I'm a Computer Science undergraduate in my 7th semester at the University of Engineering and Technology, Peshawar. Over the last few semesters I've focused on three areas that I genuinely enjoy: full-stack web development, applied AI & machine learning, and cybersecurity.

I've designed, built, and deployed multiple live projects by myself — from a production MERN e-commerce storefront (GemsInsider), to an explainable-AI crop disease detector (CropGuard AI), to an AI business command center (OpsPulse). On each project I handle the architecture, databases, APIs, styling, hosting, and deployment myself.

As a Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH), security is a core part of how I build, not an afterthought. I think in terms of JWT auth, safe input handling, and hardening from day one — which is unusual at the junior level and shows up in the quality of my work.

I'm a quick learner and a self-starter. I'm looking for junior developer, AI/ML, or security roles where I can grow under senior engineers while shipping clean, reliable, and responsive results on schedule.
